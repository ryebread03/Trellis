Trellis

Projects as a tree, not a timeline

Trellis is a personal project manager for work without deadlines. You break each project into tasks and connect them by what depends on what. Trellis then shows you what is ready to start next.

What it does

  • Draws each project as a flowchart of tasks and their dependencies, with subtasks nested up to four levels deep.
  • Marks a task ready once everything it depends on is done.
  • Plans your week across projects and tracks your momentum over time.
  • Keeps each project's notes and files in an ordinary folder on your computer.

Where your data lives

Trellis is a desktop app with no server of its own. Your projects are saved on your computer. If you choose, Trellis can also sync them through your own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox account. When it does, it only uses a folder named Trellis that it creates itself. See the privacy policy for details.
