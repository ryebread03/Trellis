Trellis

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Trellis is a desktop app for managing personal projects. Trellis has no server, collects no analytics, and sends your data to nobody. This page explains what Trellis does with your data, including what it gets from Google, Microsoft or Dropbox when you connect one of them.

Data stored on your computer

Your projects, tasks, notes and files are saved in folders on your own computer. They stay there unless you choose to sync them. The developer of Trellis cannot access them.

Cloud sync (optional)

You can tell Trellis to sync your projects through your own Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive or Dropbox account. When you sign in, Trellis talks to that provider directly from your computer. No other server is involved.

  • Google Drive. Trellis requests only the drive.file permission. This lets it see and change only the files and folders Trellis itself created, which is a folder named "Trellis" in your My Drive. It cannot see any of your other Drive files. Trellis also reads your Google account's name and email address, and only uses them to tell your accounts apart in Trellis's own settings.
  • OneDrive and Dropbox. Trellis uses an app-only folder (Apps › Trellis) and cannot see files outside it. It reads your account name and email for the same reason as above.

The sign-in tokens these services issue are encrypted with your operating system's secure storage and saved on your computer. They are never sent anywhere except back to the provider that issued them.

Google user data

Trellis uses data from Google only to sync your Trellis projects between your own devices. It does not sell or share that data. It does not use it for advertising. It does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models, and it does not transfer it to anyone else. Trellis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

To remove Trellis's access, sign out in Trellis's Settings or revoke it from your account (for Google, at myaccount.google.com/permissions). To delete your data, delete the Trellis folder from your computer and from your cloud storage.
